How it works

Start with a free trial class.

For many little ones this is their first group activity, so you can stay with your child the whole time. We work in small groups at our nursery on ul. Dąbrowskiego 32/1U.

  1. 1

    Give us a call

    Tell us how old your child is and we'll pick one class from the schedule together.

  2. 2

    Come for a trial

    The first class is free. See what it's like and whether your little one feels at home.

  3. 3

    Sign up for a month

    If you like it, you sign up for a month and receive the schedule by email.

  4. 4

    Join the classes

    Come to all the classes in the schedule between 9:00 and 12:00.

Good to know

  • Payment in advance, by the 5th of each month.
  • The agreement runs for the school year, with one month's notice.
  • The fee is monthly, so individual absences don't reduce it.
  • Before the first class, please tell us about any allergies or contraindications, e.g. related to animals.
